How the pack is organized

Find your scout's den

A pack is made of dens, and a den is a group of scouts in the same grade. Dens meet on their own through the month, and the whole pack comes together at the pack meeting.

If your family signed on partway through the year, you have not missed anything you cannot catch up on. Here is the short version.

Two kinds of meetings

Den meetings are your scout's own grade, run by the den leader. Pack meetings bring every den together, usually once a month.

The uniform

Scouts wear a uniform shirt with the pack number and rank insignia. The Resources page covers what to buy and where each patch goes.

Parents come along

Cub Scouts is a family program. An adult stays for den and pack meetings, and campouts are for the whole family.

A Scout is

The twelve points of the Scout Law. Cub Scouts learn them by heart, and most parents pick them up along the way.

  • Trustworthy
  • Loyal
  • Helpful
  • Friendly
  • Courteous
  • Kind
  • Obedient
  • Cheerful
  • Thrifty
  • Brave
  • Clean
  • Reverent
